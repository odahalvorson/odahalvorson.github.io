About PingLoop

Last updated: 2026.09.16

PingLoop is an uptime and API monitoring service for small engineering teams. It watches the services you depend on and tells you the moment something stops working, so problems get fixed before customers run into them.

Why we're building it

Most monitoring tools are built for companies with a dedicated platform team. If you're a two-person startup or a freelancer managing a dozen client sites, you end up either paying for features you'll never use or checking things manually. PingLoop is a deliberately narrow product: reliable checks, clear alerts, and a status page you can share — nothing else.

Where we are today

PingLoop is currently in active development. The core monitoring engine and alerting pipeline are working, and we're onboarding a small group of early users to shape the product before a wider release.
